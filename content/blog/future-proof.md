## The Fall is Real

I graduated college in 2011, with my BS in Computer Science.

We were the future. A golden ticket. We we're told "We'll always need technology". Big tech was emptying their pockets to attract everyone. Starting salaries of six-figures. Eye watering benefits. "Learn to code"

While I wasn't part of Bay Area extravaganza, things we're still good in my home state. Employers were hiring devs constantly. Switching jobs was an easy 10-25% gain in salary and title. A degree and some experience was all you needed.

Just 15 years later my degree feels practically worthless. The skills and expertise i've accumlulated seem inconsequintial. AI has made my path irrelevant.

This isn't a rail against AI. Its here. I use it to do my work. It's an invaluable tool. I just don't think anyone was prepared for the field to change this fast.

Keep up with all the news you want. All the youtube videos you watch. All the tutorials you can read. It won't be enough.
