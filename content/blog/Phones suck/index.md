I'm admittedly not a phone power user. In fact I kind of hate them. Other than email, texting, and some social media I don't use it for much of anything. I recently upgraded from my Galaxy S22 to a Pixel 10 and I have to say...its underwhelming. Replacing a 4 old piece of technology just feels like I have better battery life now. Thats about it. Whos buying these things? Why are they now upwards of 1000 dollars. Who gives a shit about this slab of glass? Is the camera really better? Is the screen really brighter? Is 45w vs 25w charging really changing peoples lives? I have to charge it less...thats about all I got out of this.
